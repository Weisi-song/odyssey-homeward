# 《奥德赛：归乡》

一场互动式映前导读：你将以奥德修斯的身份，走完特洛伊战争之后持续十年的海上归途。

## 体验内容

- 从特洛伊启程，沿航海地图推进完整旅程
- 进入独眼巨人、风袋、喀耳刻、冥界、塞壬与海峡等关键篇章
- 在故事节点作出选择，并理解人物、神祇与命运之间的关系
- 通过场景插画、人物卡片、声音和动态交互建立叙事氛围
- 最终返回伊萨卡，完成属于自己的归乡记录

## 项目定位

这不是对原著的逐章复述，而是一次面向观影前体验的叙事设计实验：用第一人称选择和空间化旅程，帮助没有读过《奥德赛》的用户快速建立人物、事件与主题之间的联系。

## 公开范围

本仓库为可直接浏览的产品演示构建版本，主要用于展示交互与视觉体验。

## English

# Odyssey: Homeward

An interactive pre-film introduction in which you take the role of Odysseus and travel through the ten-year voyage that followed the Trojan War.

## Experience

- Depart from Troy and move through the full journey on a navigable voyage map
- Enter key episodes including the Cyclops, the bag of winds, Circe, the Underworld, the Sirens, and the strait
- Make choices at narrative moments and understand the relationships among mortals, gods, and fate
- Build narrative atmosphere through illustrated scenes, character cards, sound, and motion
- Return to Ithaca and complete a personal record of the homeward journey

## Product direction

This is not a chapter-by-chapter retelling. It is a narrative-design experiment for audiences approaching *The Odyssey* before a film: first-person choices and a spatial journey help users quickly connect the characters, events, and themes.

## Public scope

This repository contains a directly viewable demo build intended to showcase the interaction and visual experience.
